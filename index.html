What happens in a first session?+

We'll talk about what's bringing you to therapy, what you hope might change, and any questions you have. There's no pressure to share more than feels comfortable.

How do online sessions work?+

Sessions take place by secure video call. You'll need a private space, a stable internet connection and a device with a camera and microphone. Your confirmation will include joining details.

How long is a session?+

Each session is 50 minutes. Your first conversation is a chance for us to meet and see whether working together feels right for you.

Is everything confidential?+

Your privacy is important. What you share is treated confidentially, subject to applicable professional and legal safeguarding requirements. We'll talk through this together before we begin.

What if I need to reschedule?+

Just get in touch as early as you can. Add your cancellation and rescheduling policy here before opening bookings.

Can therapy help if I don't know what's wrong?+

Absolutely. You don't need a diagnosis or a clear explanation to ask for support. We can start with how things feel and take it from there.
